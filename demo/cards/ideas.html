Four ideas
01

A change is an intent, not a diff.

A goal, a declared scope of files, and an acceptance note. The diff is just the current attempt.

02

Every attempt gets its own strand.

A real Artifacts fork of trunk, with its own tokens and its own rate budget. “10,000 agents, 10,000 repos.”

03

The lease is the token.

Claiming mints a write token whose TTL is the lease. Expired or revoked, the next git push is a 403.

04

Conflicts are replayed, never merged by hand.

Disjoint work lands in parallel. Clean merges land automatically. A real conflict is redone on the new trunk.
